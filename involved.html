Run a node

LinuxAlways-on machine

Typical first tasks

  • Run a testnet node and report what broke
  • Document the setup so the next person has it easier
  • Help define what minimum hardware actually needs to be

What we can't promise

  • No mainnet yet, so no rewards and no uptime expectations.
Few hours a week

Protocol security research

Examine the security properties of the network at the protocol layer, where the assumptions everything else rests on get made.

CryptographyFormal methods

Typical first tasks

  • Review the threat model and attack the assumptions in it
  • Reproduce a known class of attack against the testnet
  • Co-author a finding for publication

What we can offer

  • Co-authorship and full publication. We don't sit on findings.
Few hours a week

Privacy research

Study how much a public ledger reveals and what can be done about it. Transparency and surveillance are separated by design choices, and this is where they get made.

Privacy engineeringData protection law

Typical first tasks

  • Map what the current design leaks and to whom
  • Compare against data protection expectations in Bangladesh
  • Propose mitigations that survive contact with real usage
An hour at a time

Policy and regulatory work

Help shape the legal frameworks blockchain adoption in Bangladesh will need. We work with government authorities, industry, and academic institutions.

Bangladeshi lawFinancial regulation

Typical first tasks

  • Review a draft submission before it goes to a consultation
  • Flag where our technical claims overreach legally
  • Brief us on a regulation we've misread

Why this route matters

  • The best protocol in the world fails if the law makes it unusable.

Teaching experienceBangla and English

Straight answers

What contributing here does and doesn't get you

What we can offer

  • Public credit for your work, by name
  • Co-authorship on research you contribute to
  • Real influence on decisions, because the team is small
  • An honest answer when we don't know something

What we can't, yet

  • Payment. This is volunteer work today. Confirm
  • A structured onboarding programme
  • Certainty that the network ships on any timeline
  • Tokens, equity, or financial return of any kind
