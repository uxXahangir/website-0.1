Getting started

Three steps in

There is no application process and no fee. Find the work that fits what you already do, then tell us.

1

Find your fit

Filter the catalogue above by what you do. Every programme lists who it is for and whether it is running yet.

2

Tell us where you'd help

Send a short note naming the programme and what you bring. Code, research, legal work, teaching, or time.

3

Start

We reply with the current state of that work, who else is on it, and where you can pick something up.

Build note. These three steps describe an intended process, not one documented in the source profile. Confirm the real onboarding path before launch, including who replies and how fast. A stated response time you cannot meet does more damage than no roadmap at all.
